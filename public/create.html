How

How players get a spot, and what you want to hear about.

First-Come First-Served
Spots fill in the order people sign up. Once it's full, everyone else waits, and if someone drops out the next person in line gets their spot automatically.
Waitlist Mode
Everyone applies and you choose who's in. Nobody is added automatically, so if someone drops out you pick their replacement.

Keep This One For Yourself

Your management link is different from the invitation. Reply "1" to the confirmation text you just received and we'll send it to you.

It opens the page where you add and remove players, add photos, send announcements and cancel the game. Because it comes by text, you can get it on any device - just reply "1" again.
