The Constraint

Six Base Colors, Each With One Job

Everything else is derived through opacity or mixing—not another named color.

Brand#166534

Primary actions, links, selection, focus, and success.

Ink#172033

Headings, body text, secondary buttons, and derived borders.

Canvas#F4F6F3

The quiet background behind every page and major section.

Surface#FFFFFF

Cards, forms, navigation, menus, and dialogs.

Warning#A85D00

Waitlists, cautions, and items that need attention.

Danger#B42318

Removal, cancellation, destructive actions, and errors.

Step Two

Review The Component Rules

The same rules apply on every page and at every screen size.

Button Hierarchy

Color communicates purpose, not decoration.

01
Primary One main action per section
Secondary Alternative or supporting action
Danger Destructive action only
Quiet Back, cancel, or dismiss

Button Sizes

Only three sizes, with a safe mobile touch target.

02
36 px Dense desktop rows
44 px Default everywhere
52 px Page-level action
Compact buttons become 44 px tall on small screens. Paired actions use equal widths, and major mobile actions span the available width.

Forms And Focus

All controls share height, radius, labels, and focus treatment.

03
Use a first and last name.
Enter a complete mobile number.

Status Language

Status colors stay small, meaningful, and readable.

Large panels remain neutral. Status appears through a badge, icon, border, or concise message instead of filling the whole interface with color.
